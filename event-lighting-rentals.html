Lighting Rental FAQ

Plan the right lighting look

Can lighting match my event colors?

Yes. Many fixtures can be configured around your palette, venue and event flow. Final placement and programming depend on the selected package and site conditions.

Does the venue need to approve lighting?

Standard lighting is widely accepted, but rigging points, trussing, power access and haze or fog use may require venue approval.
