What content can the wall display?

LED walls can support compatible video, graphics and presentation content. Provide files and playback requirements in advance so we can confirm format and operation.

Which support option should I choose?

Floor support, goal-post trussing and screen totems serve different layouts. Venue access, ceiling height, sightlines and available floor space determine the best fit.
