Frequently Asked Questions

Custom Projector Monogram FAQ

What does the $250 price include?

One video projector with stand, one custom static monogram design, positioning, setup and breakdown for up to 4 hours. Additional projection locations, designs or time are quoted separately.

Can I use names, a date or my logo?

Yes. Send your exact spelling, date or logo file after selecting the rental. Your design must be approved before production; artwork suitability and lead time are confirmed during booking.

Can the monogram go on a wall or the floor?

Yes, subject to a suitable surface and projector placement. Size, sharpness and brightness depend on distance, surface color and ambient lighting.

Are uplights or animated graphics included?

No. This is one static projected design. The image is an illustrative example; surrounding uplighting is available separately.

When do I submit the artwork?

Share your design details when we confirm availability. We will agree on an artwork deadline and proof approval before producing the final design.
