Frequently Asked Questions

Walking on Clouds FAQ

What does the $395 price include?

One low-lying dry-ice fog machine, dry ice for one featured moment lasting up to 6 minutes, an operator, setup and breakdown. Runtime and coverage depend on venue conditions.

Are the colored uplights included?

No. The illustrative image shows optional uplighting coloring the white fog. Add uplights separately or use compatible lighting already in your package.

Is this the same as a standard fog machine?

No. This effect uses dry ice to produce a blanket of fog near the floor. Standard fog and haze are intended for general atmosphere and lighting beams.

Can it run throughout the reception?

This rental covers one timed activation. Ask for a quote for additional cues or extended effects. Indoor spaces with limited air movement usually give the most controlled result.

What does the venue need to provide?

Venue approval, suitable power, ventilation and access for setup. We confirm these requirements with your venue before booking.
