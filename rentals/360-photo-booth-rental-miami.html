Frequently asked questions

Planning this rental

How is this different from the regular photo booth?

The 360 booth records motion clips using a rotating arm around a platform. The regular booth offers digital photos and an optional print configuration.

What does the rental include?

The catalog lists the rotating platform, ring-light arm, shareable video content, setup and breakdown.

Does the booth need a level surface?

Yes. The platform and moving arm require an appropriate, level placement area that is reviewed before setup.

Can it be used for branded events?

The catalog identifies branded events as an appropriate use. Exact content or branding requirements are confirmed during planning.
