Equipment details

What to know before booking

The mounted option includes two cannons. The handheld option includes one gun with a purple LED effect. Venue approval is required, and the production team confirms supply, placement and operating requirements before the event.

Delivery and breakdown

Setup is planned around the venue

For mounted cannons, positioning and surrounding clearance are reviewed before installation. The handheld gun requires an agreed operating plan. Final use depends on venue policies, access and the approved layout.

South Florida service area

Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and Monroe counties

Heat District Productions serves events across Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County and Monroe County. Travel outside the standard service area may be quoted separately. Final availability depends on the event date, venue, access and selected rental.
