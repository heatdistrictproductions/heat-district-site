What to know before booking

Special effects are subject to venue approval. Heat District reviews the intended position, nearby surfaces, ceiling conditions, access and venue policies before confirming the setup. Availability is confirmed during booking.

Delivery and breakdown

Setup is planned around the venue

The units are delivered, placed, connected and tested as part of the production setup. Cues are coordinated with the approved event plan, and the equipment is removed during breakdown.

South Florida service area

Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and Monroe counties

Heat District Productions serves events across Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County and Monroe County. Travel outside the standard service area may be quoted separately. Final availability depends on the event date, venue, access and selected rental.
