What to know before booking

Glow is the compact foundation. Pulse adds two moving heads for more movement and focal moments. District adds four moving heads and wash lights for the most complete package currently listed. Exact positions and programming depend on access and layout.

Delivery and breakdown

Setup is planned around the venue

Heat District reviews power, fixture placement, room transitions and venue rules, then installs, cables, programs and tests the selected package. Breakdown is completed after the event period.

South Florida service area

Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and Monroe counties

Heat District Productions serves events across Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County and Monroe County. Travel outside the standard service area may be quoted separately. Final availability depends on the event date, venue, access and selected rental.
