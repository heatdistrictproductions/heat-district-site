Illuminated Dance Floor Rental

Infinity Mirror Dance Floor Rental in Miami & South Florida

Illuminated mirrored panels create a deep tunnel effect beneath the dance surface, giving the floor visible dimension from across the room and a detailed look in photos and video. Six sizes are available, from 10×10 through 20×20, for different venue layouts and production plans.

What to know before booking

A clean, level setup area and adequate access from the loading point to the final placement are required. Doorways, elevators, stairs, loading schedules and room transitions are reviewed before setup. Indoor or outdoor suitability is confirmed during booking because weather exposure, surface conditions and power access affect safe placement.

Delivery and breakdown

Setup is planned around the venue

The production team delivers the panels, confirms placement with the venue plan, installs the floor and ramp edging, tests the illuminated look, and removes the system after the event. Final placement depends on venue access and layout. Availability depends on the event date, selected size and venue requirements.

South Florida service area

Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and Monroe counties

Heat District Productions serves events across Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County and Monroe County. Travel outside the standard service area may be quoted separately. Final availability depends on the event date, venue, access and selected rental.

Frequently asked questions

Planning this rental

Can the floor colors coordinate with my event?

Pink, blue and purple looks are commonly discussed, and other coordinated lighting looks may be possible. Final programming is confirmed with the event plan.

Can the infinity floor be installed outdoors?

Indoor or outdoor suitability is confirmed during booking after the surface, cover, weather exposure, power and access are reviewed.

How do I choose a floor size?

Start with the available setup area and the surrounding DJ, lighting, seating and traffic layout. Heat District can review the venue plan before confirming the size.

Does the price include setup?

The visible options include delivery, setup, ramp edging, testing and breakdown within the standard service area, subject to the booking and venue review.
