Equipment details

What to know before booking

The 5×8 goal-post option is mounted inside a 10-foot-high by 14-foot-wide Global Truss F34 structure. The floor-supported version keeps its support behind the same 5×8 display. The totem option uses two 8-foot truss towers with one slim 1×8 LED screen on each.

Delivery and breakdown

Setup is planned around the venue

Venue access, ceiling conditions, available floor area, power and content connections are reviewed before placement. The team delivers, assembles, connects and tests the selected display, then completes breakdown. Final placement depends on venue access and layout.

South Florida service area

Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and Monroe counties

Heat District Productions serves events across Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County and Monroe County. Travel outside the standard service area may be quoted separately. Final availability depends on the event date, venue, access and selected rental.

Frequently asked questions

Planning this rental

What can appear on the LED wall?

Logos, DJ visuals, slideshows, presentations and other prepared event content can be discussed. File delivery and playback requirements are confirmed during planning.

Which LED wall configuration should I choose?

The goal-post wall creates a framed production structure, while the floor-supported wall keeps the support behind the screen. Totems provide two narrow vertical displays.

Is trussing included?

The goal-post and LED-totem options include the specific truss structures named in the catalog. The floor-supported wall does not require overhead goal-post trussing.

Is setup included?

Yes. The visible catalog descriptions include processor, cabling, delivery, setup and breakdown for the selected configuration.
