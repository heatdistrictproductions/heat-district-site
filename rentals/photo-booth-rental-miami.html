What to know before booking

The digital-only option focuses on shareable files. The Digital + Print option adds physical printouts. Final background details, access, electrical needs and placement are confirmed during booking.

Delivery and breakdown

Setup is planned around the venue

The booth, background and props are delivered and arranged before guest use. Heat District reviews the available footprint and access route, tests the selected sharing or printing workflow, and removes the setup after the event.

South Florida service area

Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and Monroe counties

Heat District Productions serves events across Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County and Monroe County. Travel outside the standard service area may be quoted separately. Final availability depends on the event date, venue, access and selected rental.

Are props included?

Yes. Both catalog options list premium props.

How much room does the booth need?

Final placement depends on the booth footprint, background, guest queue, venue access and room layout. Those requirements are reviewed before setup.
