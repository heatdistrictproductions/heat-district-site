Special Effects FAQ

Safe, venue-ready planning

Does my venue need to approve effects?

Yes. Cold sparks, fog, CO2 and other effects require venue approval. Rules vary based on the property, location and event conditions.

Can effects be used outdoors?

Some effects may work outdoors, but wind, weather, surface conditions, power and local rules matter. We will review the event details before confirming.
