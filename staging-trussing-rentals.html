South Florida Production Structures

Trussing for lighting and LED builds

Heat District installs event trussing throughout Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and Monroe counties for compatible lighting, LED displays and visual production. Every structure must be planned around usable dimensions, ceiling height, load-in access, surface conditions, equipment loads and venue rules. Totems and goal-post builds are selected for the required visual footprint—not simply by event size.

Trussing Rental FAQ

Plan a safe production structure

What venue details are needed?

We need usable dimensions, ceiling height, load-in access, surface conditions, power availability and venue rules before confirming a truss configuration.

Can I attach my own equipment?

Any equipment used with a truss build must be reviewed in advance. Loads, mounting hardware, placement and operation must remain within the approved production plan.
