Rental details

Simple 24-hour pricing

Every listed price is for one chair or one table for a 24-hour rental. Delivery, placement, setup, breakdown, linens, and extended rental time are quoted separately according to the order, event location, access, and requested service.

Planning quantities

Match the inventory to your guest count

Chair quantities normally follow the final seated guest count plus any vendor or ceremony needs. A 6-foot rectangular table typically seats 6 to 8 guests, while a 4-foot table is useful for smaller seating arrangements, food, gifts, displays, or event check-in.

South Florida service area

Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and Monroe counties

Heat District Productions serves events across Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County and Monroe County. Travel and delivery costs depend on the venue, order size, access, and event schedule. Final inventory availability is confirmed during booking.

Frequently asked questions

Planning table and chair rentals

How long is the rental?

The displayed chair and table prices cover a 24-hour rental period.

Are delivery and setup included?

No. Delivery, placement, setup, and breakdown are quoted separately based on the event location, quantities, access, and requested service.

How many guests fit at a 6-foot table?

A 6-foot rectangular table typically seats 6 to 8 guests, depending on the chair spacing and room layout.

Can I mix chair and table options?

Yes. Add one option and quantity to your build, then return to the selector and add the other chairs or tables you need.
